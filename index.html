Yue Liu

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the mathematics department at Duke University as a part of the Multiscale Immune Systems Modeling (MISM) centre, working on modelling viral dynamics and the immune system.
My mathematical interest includes dynamical systems, PDE and agent-based modelling of biological systems, parameter inference, control theory, and equation learning.

Previously, I completed my Doctor of Philosophy (DPhil) at the Mathematical Institute in the University of Oxford. I worked with Prof. Ruth Baker and Prof. Philip Maini on the modelling of biological systems. I then conducted postdoctoral research with Dr. Alexandria Volkening on biological pattern formation and agent-based modelling at Purdue University.

Before that, I have completed a Master of Science (MSc) at University of British Columbia, supervised by Prof. Leah Edelstein-Keshet.
